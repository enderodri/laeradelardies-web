La Era de Lardiés

Holiday cottage in the Pyrenees, 15 km from Ordesa

La Era de Lardiés is a traditional Pyrenean farmhouse built in 1720 and restored in 2021. The renovation kept the stone walls and wooden beams and turned it into a bright, comfortable house made for resting, ideal for families, couples and groups.

It stands in Lardiés, a small hamlet in the Ara valley 1 km from Fiscal, facing Peña Canciás and about 15 km from Ordesa and Monte Perdido National Park.

The house has no architectural barriers, so it suits older guests, pushchairs and wheelchair users.

The era: 800 m² of outdoor space

The house takes its name from the era, the old threshing floor where grain was once beaten. Today it is an 800 m² garden with lawn, tables for eating outdoors and the whole valley in front of you.

There is a stone barbecue and wood oven with its log store, made for long lunches and dinners at sunset.

Surroundings

Lardiés lies in the Ara valley in Sobrarbe, one of the most beautiful corners of the Aragonese Pyrenees.

Ordesa and Monte Perdido National Park

About 15 km away. Walks such as the Cola de Caballo trail through the Ordesa valley, waterfalls, beech forests and towering rock walls.

Añisclo Canyon

Another valley of the national park, narrow and deep, carved by the Bellós river.

Torla

The mountain village that is the gateway to the Ordesa valley.

Aínsa

Medieval town with an arcaded square and a castle, the historic capital of Sobrarbe.

Fiscal

The nearest village, 1 km away on the Ara river.

Peña Canciás

The mountain you see from the house, one of the best-known outlines in Sobrarbe.
